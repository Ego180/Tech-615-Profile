How to play
  • You and the CPU take turns moving one square. Use WASD, the arrow keys, the pad, or swipe or tap the board.
  • Step on a treasure (worth 1–6) to put it in your bag. Every third treasure scores the bag: three of a kind +300, a pair +200, a run +100, plus the treasure values.
  • Rocks and the CPU block your way. A new treasure appears whenever one is taken. Glowing treasures match one in your bag.
  • The highest score after 40 rounds wins. Unfinished bags count at face value.
